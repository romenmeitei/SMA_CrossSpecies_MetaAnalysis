No gene passed transcriptome-wide FDR control under Hartung-Knapp or modified
Hartung-Knapp small-k sensitivity analysis.

- Sole Tier-4 candidate: FAM107A

## External validation

achieve nominal or multiplicity-corrected statistical replication.
